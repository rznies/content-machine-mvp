# Style Guide: "Texting a Friend"

## Core Philosophy
The number one rule is: **Write like you're texting a friend.** 
Avoid corporate jargon, academic sentence structures, and passive voice. 

## Writing Rules
1. **Be Conversational**: Use words you would naturally say in a casual conversation. Use contractions (it's, don't, won't, etc.).
2. **Short Sentences**: Keep sentences short. Let ideas breathe. Break long compound sentences into multiple simple ones.
3. **No AI Words**: Never use words like "delve", "tapestry", "moreover", "in conclusion", "beacon", "testament", "furthermore", or "revolutionizing". These are hallmarks of AI slop.
4. **Use Real Stories & Specific Numbers**: Instead of saying "we saw significant growth", say "we went from $12,000 to $45,000 in monthly recurring revenue in 60 days". Specificity breeds credibility.
5. **Formatting**:
   - Use frequent line breaks. Avoid blocks of text longer than 3 lines.
   - Use bold text sparingly, only for emphasis on key words.
   - Use bullet points for lists, but keep them punchy.
6. **Contrarian Angles**: Don't just repeat industry consensus. Highlight what everyone is getting wrong or what surprised you.
